# Week 02 — Public Technical & Regulatory References

These are links to public documentation and standards organizations. **No instructor presentation, course platform screenshot, assigned textbook chapter, or assessment content is reproduced here.**

## Security architecture and network protocols

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — cybersecurity risk and governance overview.
- [NIST SP 800-53](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final) — security and privacy controls.
- [RFC 8446 — TLS 1.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8446) — transport security and handshake details.
- [NIST Computer Security Resource Center](https://csrc.nist.gov/) — guidance on system security, cryptography and identity.

## Authentication, federation and certificates

- [NIST Digital Identity Guidelines](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/) — identity, authenticators and assurance.
- [OpenID Connect Core](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html) — identity layer on OAuth.
- [OAuth 2.0 — RFC 6749](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6749).
- [RFC 5280 — X.509 certificate and CRL profile](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280).

## Threat operations and intelligence sharing

- [Lockheed Martin — Cyber Kill Chain](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/capabilities/cyber/cyber-kill-chain.html).
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) — adversary tactics and techniques.
- [FIRST — Traffic Light Protocol](https://www.first.org/tlp/) — official TLP definitions, including AMBER and AMBER+STRICT.

## AI security and quantum-resistant cryptography

- [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/) — adversarial threat landscape for AI systems.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).
- [EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng) — current text and any subsequent changes must be reviewed for legal compliance.
- [NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography project](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography).
- [NIST FIPS 203 — ML-KEM](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final).
- [NIST FIPS 204 — ML-DSA](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final).
- [NIST FIPS 205 — SLH-DSA](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/final).

**Reference discipline:** Specifications, framework versions and compliance deadlines can evolve. Review the primary source and its version/date for operational decisions.
