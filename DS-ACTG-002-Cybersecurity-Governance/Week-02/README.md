# DS-ACTG-002 — Week 02: Cybersecurity Landscape — Technologies & Research Disciplines

> **Unofficial student-led learning resource.** These are independently authored notes and exercises aligned to the **topics** of Week 02, not a substitute for the course or a reproduction of the teaching materials.

## Two-session study pathway

**Session 1 — Technologies**
1. System security: privilege levels, hardware-assisted defenses, virtualization, secure boot and TPM.
2. Network security: layered protocols, TLS 1.3, firewalls/DMZ, signature- and anomaly-based intrusion detection.
3. Cryptography, key lifecycle management, PKI, X.509 certificates, and the limits of certificate trust.
4. Authentication and IAM: passwords, MFA, federation, OAuth/OIDC/SAML, RBAC and ABAC.

**Session 2 — Research disciplines**
5. Cyber operations, the seven-stage Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATT&CK tactics/techniques and technical/operational/strategic threat intelligence.
6. Defensive/offensive uses of AI, attacks on AI systems, and AI Act risk concepts.
7. The quantum threat to classical public-key cryptography and the rationale for post-quantum migration.
