# DS-ACTG-001 — Week 02: Legal and Regulatory Framework

> Unofficial, independently authored study material. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the university.

## Learning pathway

1. [Study notes](study-notes.md) — history of European privacy/data-protection law; institutions, enforcement and courts; how GDPR interacts with adjacent EU instruments.
2. [Original practice scenarios](practice-questions.md) — independent exercises with expandable explanations, **not** course assessment questions.
3. [Primary public legal sources](references.md) — official legislative texts, case law and institutional resources.

**Study method:** Read → explain distinctions without notes → solve independent questions → verify against primary legal texts.

The notes follow the **topic outline** of the Week 2 module and are written in our own words. Instructor slides, annotated presentations, course reading files and graded assessment questions are **not redistributed**. Some legal rules are simplified for teaching; consult primary sources for precise application.
